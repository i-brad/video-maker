"Aryan, dinner lag gaya, aa jao" — kitchen se
teesri baar. Awaaz pehli baar se thodi upar,
lekin abhi bhi normal.

Sofa pe baitha Aryan tablet mein colouring game
khel raha hai. Ek finger screen pe, doosra haath
cushion ko daba rakha hai. Usne suna. Bilkul
suna. Bas hila nahi. "Aryan! Suna nahi kya
maine?" Is baar awaaz mein ek dab hai jo pehle
nahi tha. Koi jawab nahi. Screen pe ek cartoon
paani mein gir raha hai, aur wo dekh raha hai wo
doobega ya nahi.

Priya — Aryan ki maa — chimte se kadhai hilate
hue apne aap se sochti hai, ye baccha sunta kyun
nahi. Aur ye sawal din mein teen baar aata hai.
Kabhi subah juton ke liye, kabhi shaam homework
ke liye, ab dinner ke liye. Teesri baar wo apni
jagah se hilti nahi, sirf awaaz thodi tez karti
hai. "Aryan, ab agar phir se bulana pada, tablet
band ho jayegi."

Chauthi baar wo kadhai chhod deti hai. Seedha
sofa ke paas jaati hai. Bilkul Aryan ke saamne
khadi ho jaati hai — itni paas ki uski shadow
screen pe pad jaati hai. Kuch nahi bolti, sirf
khadi rehti hai jab tak Aryan upar nahi dekhta.

Wo dekhta hai. "Dinner lag gaya hai. Haath dho
ke aa jao." Aryan tablet rakh deta hai aur chala
jaata hai.

Priya wapas kitchen mein aati hai aur khud se
kehti hai ki is baar strict awaaz mein bola,
isliye kaam ho gaya. Yehi soch har dusre ghar
mein hoti hai — ki chauthi baar isliye chala
kyunki hum zyada gusse mein the. Lekin agar
dhyan se dekha jaaye, gussa uss chauthi baar
mein sabse kam tha. Jo asal mein badla, wo kuch
aur tha — aur wo cheez itni chhoti hai ki
zyadatar log usko dekh hi nahi paate, aur jab
dekh lete hain to sharam aati hai ki itni simple
baat pehli baar mein kyun nahi ki.

Priya jaisi maa akeli nahi hain. Ye scene kai
gharon mein roz raat ko chalta hai — subah ka
version alag hoga, shaam ka alag, lekin pattern
wahi. Teen baar bulaana, phir apni hi awaaz se
dar jaana ki ye kahin bachche ki aadat toh nahi
ban rahi. Aur jab kaam ho jaata hai, dil mein ye
laga rehta hai ki shayad awaaz mein wo cheez thi
jo dar paida karti hai, aur wahi cheez baar baar
use karni padegi. Ye sabse zyada tired kar dene
wali baat hai — roz thoda zyada zor lagana, aur
phir bhi na jaanna ki kal kya kaam karega.

Ab yahin se samajhte hain, pehli teen baar kaam
kyun nahi aaya, aur chauthi baar kyun aaya —
bina chillaye. Jab hum kitchen se, doosre kamre
se, ya sofa ke us paar se bolte hain, baccha
sunta to hai, lekin uska dhyan usko "zaroori"
instruction nahi maanta. Jo awaaz door se aati
hai wo background noise jaisi lagti hai — TV
chal rahi hai, bahar horn baj raha hai, aur ab
ek awaaz "khaana khao" bhi usi list mein aa
jaati hai. Teeno ek jaisi priority pe rehte hain
jab tak koi cheez unhe alag nahi karti.

Jo unhe alag karti hai — paas jaana, seedha
saamne khade hona, aur uski aankhon tak
pahunchna jab tak wo dekh na le. Chhoti si baat
lagti hai, lekin jab parent bachche ke bilkul
paas, uski height ke barabar aata hai, aur ek hi
instruction deta hai, tab baat maanne ka chance
sabse zyada badh jaata hai. Isme gusse ka koi
role nahi hai. Awaaz tez karna kaam nahi karta —
paas jaana karta hai.

Agar aapke ghar mein bhi yehi hota hai — teen
baar bulao, chauthi baar khud jaana pade —
comment mein bas ek line likh dena, "mere ghar
mein bhi." Padh ke pata chalega kitne ghar isi
shaam se guzar rahe hain. Aur ye sirf maa ka
kaam nahi hai — jo bhi ghar mein wo awaaz lagata
hai, wahi ye try kar sakta hai. Baat ye nahi ki
bulane wala kaun hai, baat ye hai ki bulawa
kahan se aa raha hai.

Ek doosra ghar dekhte hain. Neha apne bete Kabir
ko subah school ke liye taiyar kar rahi hai.
"Kabir, shoes pehno." Kabir doosre kamre mein
remote dhundh raha hai. Kuch response nahi.
"Kabir! Shoes!" Kabir aata hai, ek shoe pehenta
hai, phir wapas remote ki taraf mud jaata hai.

Neha ruk jaati hai. Wo Kabir ke paas jaati hai,
uske saamne baith jaati hai — ussi height pe,
jahan uski aankhein Kabir ki aankhon se milti
hain. "Kabir, dono shoes ab pehno, phir bus aa
jayegi." Ek hi baat, ek hi baar. Kabir dono
shoes pehen leta hai.

Bahar school bus wala teesri baar horn baja
chuka hai, aur Neha ki maa — Kabir ki nani —
dalaan mein khadi ye sab dekh rahi thi. Wo kuch
nahi bolti, lekin unke chehre pe wo hairaani hai
jo har bar dikhti hai jab koi cheez itni aasaani
se ho jaaye jo pehle roz ki ladai thi. Farq sirf
ye tha ki Neha khadi nahi rahi, awaaz oonchi
nahi ki — wo neeche gayi, paas gayi, aur wait
kiya jab tak Kabir ka dhyan usi pe tha. Yahi
cheez sabse zyada log miss karte hain. Hum
sochte hain baccha jaanbojh kar ignore kar raha
hai, jab asal mein uska dhyan kisi aur jagah
busy hai, aur humari awaaz uss dhyan ko todh
nahi paa rahi jab tak hum khud uss jagah nahi
jaate jahan uska dhyan hai.

Ek teesra ghar. Rajesh apne bete Vivaan, das
saal ke, se roz raat ko yehi ladai lad rahe hain
— "TV band karo, so jao." Kamra andhera hai,
sirf TV ki neeli roshni Vivaan ke chehre pe pad
rahi hai. Rajesh sofa ke doosre kone se bolte
hain, apni phone se aankhein bhi nahi uthate.
Vivaan sunta hai, "haan papa," aur wahi baitha
rehta hai. Paanch minute baad wahi baat, phir
wahi jawaab, jaise ek recording chal rahi ho.

Rajesh ek raat try karte hain kuch alag. Wo
phone side pe rakhte hain, TV ke saamne jaate
hain, Vivaan ki nazar se apni nazar milate hain,
aur bolte hain — "TV band, ab so jao." Vivaan
khud remote uthaata hai.

Rajesh baad mein kehte hain unko lagta tha unka
beta jaanbojh kar unhe ignore kar raha tha. Asal
mein Vivaan ne kabhi confirm hi nahi kiya tha ki
baat uske liye thi — kyunki koi bhi cheez jo
door se aaye, bina eye contact ke, dimaag usko
"shayad kisi aur ke liye" ki category mein daal
deta hai. Aur kabhi kabhi sach mein aisa hi hota
hai — baccha jhooth nahi bol raha jab wo kehta
hai "maine suna nahi." TV ka cartoon apni chhoti
duniya bana leta hai, colouring game ki screen
apni jagah, aur ek awaaz jo kamre ke us kone se
aa rahi hai, usme utni pakad nahi hoti jitni us
screen mein hai. Ye baccha budtameez nahi hai —
uska dhyan bas kahin aur laga hua hai, aur
humari zimmedari hai wahan tak jaana jahan wo
dhyan hai, na ki wahin se chillaana jahan hum
khade hain.

Isse ye bhi nahi matlab ki har baat ke liye
uthna padega ya ghutne ke bal baithna padega.
Bas teen chhoti cheezein: door se mat bolo, paas
jao. Sirf tab bolo jab uski aankhein tumhari
taraf ho. Aur ek hi baar bolo — dobara mat kaho,
kyunki dobara kehna uska dimaag sikhata hai ki
pehli baat optional thi. Us raat Priya ne yehi
try kiya. Dinner ke waqt kitchen se nahi bulaya.
Wo sofa tak gayi, Aryan ke saamne baithi, uska
naam liya — "Aryan" — aur ruk gayi jab tak usne
upar dekha. Phir sirf ek baat: "Dinner ready
hai." Aryan uthaa, tablet side pe rakha, aur
chala gaya. Koi teesri baar nahi, koi chauthi
baar nahi. Priya thodi der sofa pe hi baithi
rahi. Usse laga jaise usne kuch bada nahi kiya —
bas jagah badli thi. Lekin ghar mein wo shaam
sabse shaant thi jo unhone kaafi dino mein dekhi
thi.

Isme koi naya rule nahi hai, koi nayi saza nahi
hai. Sirf jagah badalna hai — kitchen se sofa
tak, doorway se bache ke saamne tak. Aur yahi
wajah hai ki zyadatar log ise try hi nahi karte,
kyunki lagta hai itni chhoti si baat kaam kaise
karegi. Wahi cheez jo sabse zyada simple lagti
hai, wahi sabse zyada miss ho jaati hai. Yahi ek
move hai jo aaj raat try kar sakte hain. Jab
baccha sunta na lage, door se mat bolo. Uske
paas jao. Uski height tak aao. Wait karo jab tak
uski aankhein tumhari taraf ho. Phir sirf ek
baar bolo. Agar aaj raat aap ye try karein aur
farak dikhe, ya na dikhe, comment mein likh dena
kal subah ki kya hua. Aur kal isi waqt hum baat
karenge ki jab ek baar kehne ke bawajood baccha
phir bhi nahi sunta, tab kya karna hai — kyunki
wo agli problem hai jo isi ghar mein kal shuru
hogi. Milte hain kal, isi jagah.
